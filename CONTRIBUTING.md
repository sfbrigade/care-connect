# Contributing

This project is being built in partnership with the City and County of San Francisco's Mayor's Office of Innovation. All contributers must sign a volunteer agreement with the City and County of San Francisco before contributions to this repository can be accepted. Please do not submit any pull requests before signing a volunteer agreement or they will be immediately closed. At this time, this agreement is limited to United States residents only.

To request a volunteer agreement, please join SF Civic Tech's Weekly Civic Hack Night Zoom meeting held Wednesdays at 6:30pm Pacific Time to introduce yourself and learn more about this project. Regular attendance at this meeting is also required to contribute to this project. For more information about Hack Night, please visit: https://www.sfcivictech.org/
